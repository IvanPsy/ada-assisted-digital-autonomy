# Template: Task card

For breaking an assignment (an essay, a set of exercises, a study session, a project) into steps the learner can own. Built on signature A, **and only for signature A functions** (planning, sequencing, organizing): the card exists in four versions, from most supported to least, and the learner moves down the levels over time.

## Request to the co-pilot

```text
Please make a task card for this assignment.
Learner needs (from profile sheet): [e.g. getting started, ordering steps, time]
Current support level (1-4): [...]
Assignment: [paste or describe]
Time available: [...]
```

## Output structure

```markdown
### Task card: [assignment in plain words]

**Goal:** [what "done" looks like, in one sentence]
**Time:** [total] · **Break after:** [minutes]

| # | Step | Who decides | My notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [first concrete action, starting with a verb] | me | |
| 2 | [...] | me / with help | |
| … | | | |

**Checkpoint:** after step [n], stop and check: [one concrete question]
**If I get stuck:** [one small next action, not the solution]
```

## The four levels

| Level | What the card gives | What the learner decides |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The card proposes, the learner validates** | All steps written, in order | Confirms, changes or removes steps |
| **2. The card offers options** | Steps written, order left open, or 2-3 alternative first steps | Chooses the order and the starting point |
| **3. The learner proposes, the card gives feedback** | Only goal, time and a blank table; a short checklist to review the learner's plan | Writes all the steps |
| **4. The learner decides, the tool executes** | Nothing; the learner asks for what they need (a timer, a reminder, a format) | Everything epistemic |

Move one level at a time, tell the learner before, and step back without stigma during exams or hard periods. Do not use these levels for access supports (reading, decoding, transcription): those do not move down.

## Notes

- If the assignment involves heavy reading or writing, pair the card with an [accessible text](accessible-text.md). The card fades; the text access does not.
- A card that the learner never edits is at the wrong level, or is being used as a checklist to obey instead of a plan to own. Ask them.
